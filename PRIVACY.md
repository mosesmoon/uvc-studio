# UVC Studio Privacy Policy

[English](#english) · [台灣正體中文](#台灣正體中文)

Effective date: October 11, 2026. Applies to UVC Studio for Meta Quest, Linux and Windows, published by Moses-Studio.

## English

**In short:** UVC Studio does not collect, store or send any personal data. Everything it does happens on your device.

### What UVC Studio does not do

- It has no account and no sign-in.
- It does not connect to the internet itself, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
- It does not upload your picture, sound, snapshots or recordings anywhere.

### Data that stays on your device

- **Settings** (language, theme, picture options and so on) are saved in the app's own storage on your device.
- **Snapshots and recordings** are saved only when you take them: in Pictures/UVC Studio and Videos (Movies)/UVC Studio. You decide what to keep, share or delete.
- **Free daily time (Meta Quest)**: how much of today's free 4K upscaling and 3D depth time you have used is kept in the app's storage on the headset.

### Permissions (Meta Quest)

- **Camera and USB camera**: Android grants access to USB capture cards and webcams only to apps that hold these permissions. UVC Studio uses them for the USB devices you plug in; the headset's own cameras are never used.
- **Microphone (record audio)**: used to read the capture card's sound and, only when you turn on *Record microphone*, to mix your voice into a recording. The sound is processed on the headset and never sent anywhere.
- **Hand tracking**: lets you use the app with your hands. Hand data is handled by the headset's system; UVC Studio only receives pointer and pinch input.

### Purchases on Meta Quest

UVC Studio Platinum is sold as an in-app purchase through the Meta Horizon Store. When the app starts, it asks the headset's Meta Horizon Store service whether you own UVC Studio and whether you bought Platinum, and it shows the store's price. The purchase itself, your payment details and your Meta account are handled by Meta under [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/); UVC Studio does not receive or store them and keeps no record of the purchase beyond unlocking the features.

### Children

UVC Studio does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

### Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/mosesmoon/uvc-studio/issues>.

## 台灣正體中文

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日。適用於 Moses-Studio 發行的 UVC Studio（Meta Quest、Linux 與 Windows 版）。

**重點：** UVC Studio 不蒐集、不儲存也不傳送任何個人資料，所有處理都在你的裝置上進行。

### UVC Studio 不會做的事

- 沒有帳號，也不需要登入。
- App 本身不連網，不含任何分析、廣告或追蹤程式。
- 不會把你的畫面、聲音、截圖或錄影上傳到任何地方。

### 只留在你裝置上的資料

- **設定**（語言、主題、畫面選項等）存在裝置上 App 自己的儲存空間。
- **截圖與錄影**只在你按下時才儲存，存到「圖片／UVC Studio」與「影片／UVC Studio」。要保留、分享或刪除，都由你決定。
- **每日免費時間（Meta Quest）**：當天已使用多少 4K 升頻與 3D 景深免費時間，記錄在頭顯上 App 的儲存空間。

### 權限（Meta Quest）

- **相機與 USB 相機**：Android 只讓具備這些權限的 App 存取 USB 擷取卡與網路攝影機。UVC Studio 只用在你插上的 USB 裝置，絕不使用頭顯本身的鏡頭。
- **麥克風（錄音）**：用來讀取擷取卡的聲音；只有在你開啟「錄製麥克風」時，才會把你的聲音混入錄影。聲音只在頭顯上處理，不會傳送到任何地方。
- **手部追蹤**：讓你用雙手操作。手部資料由頭顯系統處理，UVC Studio 只接收指標與捏合的操作。

### Meta Quest 上的購買

「UVC Studio 白金」透過 Meta Horizon Store 以 App 內購買販售。App 啟動時，會向頭顯的 Meta Horizon Store 服務確認你是否擁有 UVC Studio、是否購買過白金，並顯示商店售價。購買本身、你的付款資料與 Meta 帳號都由 Meta 依照 [Meta 隱私政策](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/)處理；UVC Studio 不會取得或儲存這些資料，除了解鎖功能之外，也不保留任何購買紀錄。

### 兒童

UVC Studio 不向任何人（包括兒童）蒐集個人資料。

### 變更與聯絡方式

本政策如有變更，會在此公布新版本並更新生效日期。如有疑問，請在 <https://github.com/mosesmoon/uvc-studio/issues> 提出 issue。
